How to make me

12 is the magic number

One spoon of ground coffee per cup — so twelve spoons for a full pot, and that's my ceiling. Water to the MAX line (never over), and I'll take it from there.

Discover more here.

Timing

Catch me before noon

Mornings I'm full, warm, and frankly magnificent. After 3pm I mostly go ignored, so I'd rather not be brewed then and left to go cold and bitter — help me stay magnificent.

When you're done

Don't leave me like that

Sit overnight with old grounds and I start growing a personality — not a good one. Tip the grounds out, give the carafe and filter a rinse, and never leave the empty jug baking on the hot plate — do that and I'll forgive you by morning.
